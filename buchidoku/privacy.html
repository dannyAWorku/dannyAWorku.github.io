Privacy Policy

Buchidoku · Last updated October 29, 2026

Buchidoku ("the game") is a puzzle game for Android and iPhone made by Daniel Alemu ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information is used when you play, and why. If you have a question, email hello@dwrlabs.com.

The short version

  • There are no accounts. We don't ask for your name, email or any other personal details.
  • Your progress, scores and settings are saved only on your device.
  • The free version shows ads from Google AdMob. Google may collect device information to show and measure ads, as described below.
  • Purchases are handled by Apple or Google. We never see your payment details.

Information stored on your device

The game saves your level progress, scores, best times, daily puzzle results, sound and vibration settings, and whether you bought "Remove ads". This stays on your device and is not sent to us. Deleting the app deletes it.

Advertising (free version)

The free version shows ads provided by Google AdMob. To show ads, limit how often you see them, measure how they perform and prevent fraud, Google may collect and use information such as:

  • your device's advertising identifier (Android Advertising ID, or Apple's IDFA if you allow tracking),
  • your IP address and approximate location derived from it,
  • device and app information (for example model, operating system and language),
  • how you interact with the ads you see.

Google's use of this information is governed by Google's own policies: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

Your choices:

  • If you are in the European Economic Area, the UK or Switzerland, the game asks for your consent before personalized ads are shown. You can change your choice anytime in the game under Settings → Ad privacy choices.
  • On iPhone, the game asks for permission before using your IDFA. You can change this in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • On Android, you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device's Google settings (Settings → Google → Ads, or Security & privacy → Privacy → Ads).
  • Buying "Remove ads" turns off all ads in the game.

Purchases

The one-time "Remove ads" purchase is processed by the Apple App Store or Google Play. They handle your payment information under their own privacy policies. We only receive confirmation that the purchase was made, so the game can turn ads off and restore the purchase on your other devices.

Children

Buchidoku is a general-audience game and is not directed at children under 13. We don't knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the game, contact us and we will help remove it.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR in Europe or privacy laws in California and other US states), you may have the right to access, correct or delete personal information, or to object to its use. Because we don't collect personal information ourselves, most requests are handled through the choices above or directly with Google. You can always email us and we will help.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date at the top.
